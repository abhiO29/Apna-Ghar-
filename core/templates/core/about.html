Our Story

About अपना घर वृद्ध आश्रम

A refuge of warmth, dignity and belonging for Delhi's elderly since our founding.

How It All Began

A door that never closes on the elderly

अपना घर वृद्ध आश्रम was started by Manav Sewa Charitable Samiti (Regd.) with a simple belief: no one should spend their final years alone, unfed, or unheard. What began as a small effort to feed and shelter a handful of abandoned and destitute elders in the Najafgarh Road area has grown into a full residential home — offering food, medical attention, clean clothing and, most importantly, a family.

Today, our building on MS Block, Ranaji Enclave houses senior citizens who have nowhere else to turn — some abandoned by families, some without any family left, and some who simply need professional, compassionate care in their later years. Every meal served, every bed made, and every festival celebrated here is made possible by the generosity of donors and volunteers like you.

Our Mission

To provide free shelter, nutritious food, healthcare and emotional support to elderly men and women who are destitute, abandoned or without adequate family care — restoring dignity to their everyday lives.

Our Vision

A society where every senior citizen ages with respect, security and love — where "अपना घर" is not just our name, but a promise every elder in Delhi can rely on.

Why Choose Us

  • Registered charitable organisation (Manav Sewa Charitable Samiti)
  • 100% of donations go towards resident welfare
  • Transparent bank & UPI donation records
  • Hands-on, family-style care — not an institution
