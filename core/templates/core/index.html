Welcome Home

Every senior deserves a home, not just shelter

अपना घर वृद्ध आश्रम is a safe, caring residence for elderly men and women who have no one to look after them, or who simply need a peaceful place to spend their later years with dignity. From nourishing meals to medical attention and companionship, we try to give every resident what the name promises — their own home.

Free shelter & meals

Basic medical care

Clean clothing & hygiene
