A New Initiative

Community Clothing Bank

Give a shirt a second life. Help a family in need dress with dignity — for just ₹10–₹20.

How It Works

Donate. Sort. Share.

Our Community Clothing Bank, run alongside अपना घर वृद्ध आश्रम, collects clean, gently-used clothes from generous donors like you. Every item is sorted by category and size, then displayed neatly on numbered shelves in our showroom.

Families in need can visit and take clothes for a small maintenance contribution — just ₹10 to ₹20 per item — which helps us keep the showroom clean, organised and running sustainably.

Per item — ₹10 to ₹20 only
Two Ways To Take Part

Donate clothes or take what you need

I Want To Donate Clothes

Drop off clean, wearable clothes for men, women or children at our showroom, or call us to arrange a pick-up. Every donation is logged so donors can be thanked and tracked transparently.

  • Clothes should be clean and in wearable condition
  • Sort by Men / Women / Kids if possible
  • Visit our office or call ahead for a pick-up
Arrange a Donation

I Need Clothes For My Family

Visit our showroom any day during working hours. Browse racks organised by category and size, and take what you need for a small ₹10–₹20 contribution per item — no questions asked.

  • Open to any family genuinely in need
  • ₹10–₹20 per item to maintain the showroom
  • Bring your family along to choose the right size
